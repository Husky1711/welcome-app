## Mission

> **Make personal growth feel like spending time with a trusted companion instead of completing another task.**

## Philosophy

Some want to read.  
Some want to exercise.  
Some want to meditate.  
Some want to study.  
Some want to become kinder.

Instead of another app reminding them what they failed to do…

They meet a companion who says:

## Companion philosophy

**Leafu never leaves** (emotional anchor).  
**The others visit.**

Think of Leafu as your best friend.  
The others are specialists Leafu introduces when they can help.

```text
User: "I want to read."
        ↓
Leafu: "Let's visit Ollie."
        ↓
Ollie appears → reading activity → Ollie leaves
        ↓
Leafu returns
```

Leafu always remains the emotional anchor.

## Core principles

### 1. Companion First

Users should feel they are spending time with a friend, not using software.

### 2. Growth Over Perfection

Celebrate effort. Never shame failure.

### 3. One Core Companion

Leafu is the lifelong companion.

### 4. Specialist Companions

Each companion has a unique purpose and joins naturally when relevant.

### 5. Personal Growth

The goal is not productivity.  
The goal is becoming a better version of yourself.

### 6. Real Life First

Companions encourage users to live better **outside** the app, not spend more time **inside** it.

### 7. One Universe

Bloom Valley is the shared home and evolves as users grow, but the user remains the center.

### 8. Same Promise, Tailored Experiences

Children, students, professionals, and families can share the same companions and promise; experiences are tailored by goals and stage — not by building three unrelated apps at once.
