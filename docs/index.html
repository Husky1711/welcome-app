Privacy Policy

Welcome App · Last updated: July 18, 2026

Welcome App is published by P Sai Prasad. This policy explains what information the app handles and the choices available to you.

Information we process

Account information

If Firebase authentication is enabled, creating an account or signing in requires:

  • Email address for account creation, sign-in, password reset, and displaying your identity.
  • Password, sent directly to Google Firebase Authentication over an encrypted connection. The app does not store it in plain text.
  • Display name and account metadata, such as account creation time, when available from Firebase.

Google processes authentication information on our behalf. Firebase may also process technical service data, such as IP address and device or request information, to operate and secure its service. See Google's Privacy Policy and Firebase Privacy and Security.

Information stored on your device

The following content is stored locally and is not uploaded to our servers in the current version:

  • Habits, targets, completion history, streaks, and archived habits
  • Reminder preferences and reminder times
  • Private notes
  • Light/dark mode, app color, and other display preferences
  • Locally cached profile information
  • Optional profile photos and custom habit images

Your account email and display name may also be cached locally to maintain your session and personalize the app.

Device permissions

The Android app may request notification permission to schedule habit reminders. On supported Android versions, it may also direct you to system settings for exact-alarm access. Reminder schedules are created and managed on your device.

You can deny notification permission or disable reminders at any time. Core habit and note features remain available.

Sharing

The app can generate a habit-streak image. Nothing is shared automatically. An image leaves the app only when you choose Share and select another app or recipient. The selected service handles the image under its own privacy policy.

How we use information

  • Create and authenticate your account
  • Send password-reset instructions when requested
  • Display your profile and personalize the app
  • Provide habits, notes, progress, insights, reminders, appearance, and sharing features
  • Maintain app security and reliability

Data sharing and sale

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We do not use advertising SDKs or analytics SDKs in the current version.

Account information is processed by Google Firebase Authentication when Firebase authentication is enabled. Local habits, notes, completion history, photos, and settings are not sent to Firebase by the app.

Information you deliberately send using email feedback, the device share interface, or another external app is handled by the service you choose.

Data retention and deletion

  • Logging out ends your authenticated session but does not delete local habits, notes, photos, or settings.
  • Clear all app data in Settings removes locally stored account cache, avatars, habits, logs, targets, notes, and preferences from that device.
  • Uninstalling the app normally removes locally stored app data from the device.
  • Firebase account data remains until the account is deleted. Contact the developer to request account deletion.

Security

Authentication requests are transmitted to Firebase using HTTPS/TLS. Local app data is stored in the app's device storage. No storage or transmission method is completely secure, but we limit collection and keep habit and note content on-device.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when app features or service providers change. The current effective date appears at the top of this page.
