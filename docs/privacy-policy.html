Overview

Welcome App is a demonstration application. This policy explains how information is handled in the current version.

Information we collect

When you sign in, your email address is stored locally on your device to keep you logged in. No personal data is sent to external servers in this version.

How we use information

  • Maintain your session until you log out

Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties. No data leaves your device.

Data retention

Logging out removes session data. Uninstalling the app removes all app data from your device.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

This policy may be updated when new features (such as cloud authentication) are added.
