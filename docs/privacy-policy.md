# Privacy Policy — Welcome App

**Last updated:** July 8, 2026

Welcome App ("the app") is a demonstration application published by the developer.

## Information we collect

In the current version, the app uses **mock authentication only**. When you sign in:

- Your email is stored **locally on your device** in app storage to keep you logged in.
- No personal data is transmitted to external servers.

## How we use information

Locally stored email is used only to:

- Display your welcome message
- Maintain your signed-in session until you log out

## Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties. No data leaves your device in this version.

## Data retention

Logging out removes locally stored session data. Uninstalling the app removes all app data from your device.

## Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes

This policy may be updated when features such as Firebase Authentication are added.

## Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the developer via the email listed on the Google Play Store listing.
