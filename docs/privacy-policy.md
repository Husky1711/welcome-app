# Privacy Policy — Welcome App

**Last updated:** July 11, 2026

Welcome App ("the app") is published by the developer.

## Information we collect

### Account authentication (Firebase)

When you create an account or sign in, we collect:

- **Email address** — used to create and manage your account
- **Password** — stored securely by Firebase Authentication (hashed; not stored in plain text by the app)

Account data is processed by **Google Firebase Authentication** on our behalf. Firebase may collect standard service data as described in [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

### App data (stored on your device)

The following stays on your device and is **not** uploaded to our servers in the current version:

- Habits and completion history
- Private notes
- App settings (theme, reminders)
- Display name shown in the app

Your email may also be cached locally to keep you signed in between app sessions.

## How we use information

- **Email** — account sign-in, password reset, and displaying your identity in the app
- **Local app data** — habits, notes, calendar, reminders, and settings for app functionality

## Data sharing

We do not sell your personal data.

Account authentication is handled by **Firebase (Google)** as our service provider. We do not share habits, notes, or other on-device content with third parties.

## Data retention

- **Account data** — retained while your Firebase account exists. Contact us to request account deletion.
- **Local app data** — removed when you log out, use "Clear all data" in Settings, or uninstall the app.

## Security

Account credentials are transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) to Firebase. Passwords are not stored in plain text on your device.

## Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes

This policy may be updated when features change. Material updates will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.

## Contact

For questions about this policy or to request account deletion, contact the developer via the email listed on the Google Play Store listing.
